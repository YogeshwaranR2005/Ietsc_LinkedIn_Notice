What is Nunnokku?

Nunnokku is a weekly sustainability observation initiative where students identify and document meaningful sustainable practices they encounter in their everyday surroundings.

Observe

Look around and identify a meaningful sustainability practice.

Capture

Take an original photograph of what you observed.

Inspire

Explain why the observation matters and how it can inspire others.

What can you observe?

  • Plastic reduction
  • Water conservation
  • Energy efficiency
  • Waste reduction
  • Reuse & recycling
  • Sustainable transportation
  • Eco-friendly alternatives
  • Responsible consumption

Think beyond these examples. If you notice something meaningful, tell us about it.

How to participate

  1. 01

    Notice

    Find a sustainability-related practice around you.

  2. 02

    Capture

    Take a clear original photograph.

  3. 03

    Write

    Prepare a short reflection of approximately 100–150 words.

  4. 04

    Submit

    Upload your photograph and write-up through the submission form.

  5. 05

    Get featured

    Selected observations will be featured weekly on the chapter's LinkedIn page.

Your 100–150 word reflection

In your write-up, cover:

  • What did you notice?
  • Where did you notice it?
  • Why is it sustainable?
  • Why did it catch your attention?
  • What can others learn from it?

Keep it authentic. We are looking for real observations from everyday life, not textbook explanations.

Also add your name, year, department, and register/roll number at the end of your write-up, so we can credit and reach you if selected.

Before submitting

  • Use your own photograph.
  • Write 100–150 words.
  • Include your name, year, department, and register/roll number.
  • Focus on a real observation.
  • Mention the location/context where appropriate.
  • Avoid photographing people without permission.
  • Make sure your submission is clear and readable.

One observation. One story. Every week.
